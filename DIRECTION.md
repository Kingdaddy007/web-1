# TTA Concept — The Hinged Elevation

## Thesis

The vertical line in the TTA identity is treated as an architectural hinge rather than a beam of light or a refractive seam. The black opening surface becomes a pair of spatial leaves. As they rotate away, five slices of one room arrive from depth and register into a complete elevation inside a warm editorial field.

## Motion law

1. The approved identity constructs through 3.05 seconds.
2. The D-axis extends into a full-height hinge.
3. The black field rotates away as two architectural leaves.
4. Five room elevations unfold from the hinge and align into one image.
5. The sliced construction retires into a seamless room plate.
6. Typography and inquiry appear only after the room has become still.

## Deliberate differences from The Axis Opens

- No WebGL, shader, refraction, Canvas UI, or permanent black environment.
- The final hero is a contained 16:10 spatial elevation on a warm plaster field, preserving the complete room at wide laptop ratios.
- Motion is CSS 3D architectural choreography controlled by one deterministic React timeline.
- The image is assembled physically rather than optically revealed.

## Fallback and performance

- Reduced motion crossfades directly from the approved logo into the final editorial composition.
- Semantic navigation, copy, and inquiry remain DOM content.
- The effect uses one image asset and compositor-friendly transforms; no realtime render loop remains after arrival.

## Truth boundary

The room plate is still a private speculative extension of observed TTA imagery. The concept does not assert project scale, awards, team size, or verified commercial claims.
