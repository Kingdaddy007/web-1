## First-impression objective

The founder should move through this recognition sequence:

1. **That is my identity.**
2. **That is my work.**
3. **I have not seen my work presented with this level of authorship.**
4. **My brand could legitimately feel this substantial.**

The opening must communicate calm authority before it communicates technical ability. The room is the primary proof. Interface, copy, and motion must withdraw around it.

## Governing visual thesis

**The axis that constructs the identity becomes the instrument that reveals spatial order.**

This is not permission to draw a glowing laser, create a left/right split template, or force the logo line to match an unrelated ceiling edge. It means the same principle—alignment, restraint, and deliberate placement—governs both the identity and the room composition.

## Composition rules

- The hero destination must have one dominant residential image or one coherently assembled view of a single room.
- Avoid a permanent 40/60 or 50/50 black-and-image split.
- Avoid three equal cards, portrait social panels, dashboard chrome, technical HUDs, calibration language, and floating metadata.
- Black may serve as arrival darkness, a shaped threshold, or controlled negative space. It must not become an automatic luxury template.
- Typography must not default to a high-contrast luxury serif, gold accents, excessive tracking, or generic words such as timeless, bespoke, elevated, or luxurious.
- Start with no navigation, CTA, project statistics, project name, location, completion date, floor area, or services.
- Do not invent any project fact. Compositional placeholder text, if used, must be visibly labelled provisional in notes and limited to one line of 4–8 words.
- The frame must remain convincing with all text hidden.
- Use actual TTA imagery. A generated extension or image-to-video source may be proposed only after the winning composition exposes a genuine crop or camera limitation.

## Three destination-frame territories

These are composition territories, not three motion demonstrations.

### Territory 1 — Full-Bleed Spatial Threshold

One room owns almost the entire viewport. The axis arrives near an existing architectural edge, light channel, doorway, join, or negative-space boundary, but does not need to sit at the centre. The frame should feel as if darkness opened onto a real interior, not as if an image was placed beside a black text panel.

**Test:** Can one source image carry immediate authority without interface support?

**Failure condition:** It resolves into a familiar left-copy/right-image hero, a wallpaper image with text over it, or a false seam match.

### Territory 2 — The Suspended Interior

The black arrival stage remains as deep spatial silence while one large cinematic room plane or panoramic crop appears as a precisely proportioned architectural volume—not a card. The plane may sit off-centre, crop beyond the viewport, or create an unusual horizon. Logo-scale geometry and the room plane should share a disciplined alignment system.

**Test:** Can negative space make the room feel more precious and dimensional without becoming a gallery template?

**Failure condition:** Rounded card styling, drop-shadow UI, a standard centred film frame, or excessive empty black with no structural pressure.

### Territory 3 — Optical Spatial Alignment

One authoritative room is represented through two or three exact, borderless spatial planes that align into one coherent final view. The resting composition must read as a room first; the technique should disappear. No process images, founder portraits, labels, coordinates, or technical overlays belong in this frame.

**Test:** Does controlled optical alignment create a distinctive spatial signature that a flat image cannot?

**Failure condition:** The image looks sliced, glitched, calibrated, or like three separate cards.

## Copy and typography gate

The first study may omit hero copy entirely. If text is needed to judge balance, use only a short provisional line and a neutral modern sans/grotesk that does not compete with the wordmark. Copywriting begins after image hierarchy and destination composition are selected.

## Canvas UI position

Canvas UI is **deferred, not discarded**.

It may become useful only if the selected composition requires one contained realtime job that still imagery, DOM/CSS masking, or pre-rendered media cannot perform convincingly. The likely qualifying job is a subtle optical/refraction seam or perspective-dependent alignment during the transition into Territory 3.

Canvas UI is not needed to design the static frames. Do not import Canvas UI, install a package, fetch upstream code, or build a shader during this stage. After a frame is selected, compare four routes for its transition:

1. still/cut;
2. DOM/CSS or SVG mask;
3. pre-rendered image-to-video media;
4. one contained Canvas/WebGL effect.

Canvas proceeds only if it communicates the selected spatial idea materially better and has a DOM-first reduced-motion/mobile fallback.
