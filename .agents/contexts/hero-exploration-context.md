# TTA Designs — Hero Exploration Context

> **Historical context:** This file governed the first hero-risk studies. Those coded studies were audited and rejected as selection candidates. Use `hero-direction-context.md` for the active direction and next gate.

## Purpose

Define the smallest truthful design context required to create three materially different opening-to-hero studies for the private TTA Designs outreach prototype. This file does not approve a final hero, transition technology, page architecture, copy line, Canvas component, or production build.

## Communication job

The opening must transform recognition of TTA's existing identity into a new perception of the studio's work:

`recognition -> curiosity -> spatial authority -> desire`

Within the first eight to twelve seconds, TTA's founder should recognize the logo and project world, then feel that the work has been given an authored digital presence that Instagram cannot provide.

The simulated prospective client should understand, without a long explanation, that refined atmosphere is supported by deliberate decisions about people, use, light, flow, material and delivery.

## First-view principles

- One dominant visual event; no competing hero anchors.
- The logo arrival and hero transition must feel like one authored sequence.
- Finished residential atmosphere leads. Process supports the argument rather than replacing the first impression.
- Existing portrait assets are a design constraint to exploit, not automatically crop away.
- Text remains sparse, selectable DOM content and visually subordinate while imagery carries attention.
- The final hero must remain compelling as a still composition.
- No generic full-screen video wallpaper, floating-card collage, beige-and-serif luxury costume, universal fade-up, loud CTA or unsupported claim.

## Asset truth

Available evidence includes portrait room films, portrait site/process films, finished-room stills, human-use stills and site-condition stills. Original wide production footage is not established. Image-to-video generation is permitted for private exploration when it preserves the source room's geometry, materials and identity. Generated movement must not invent a different project or imply that unrelated process and finished footage depict the same location.

## Provisional hero copy

Copy is a compositional placeholder, not approved founder language.

Primary working line:

> Spaces shaped around the way life is lived.

Possible annotation:

> Residential interiors by TTA Designs, Lagos.

Each study may shorten, reposition or temporarily omit the annotation. Do not replace it with generic luxury language.

## Territory A — The Line Becomes Light

**Visual thesis:** The vertical axis inside the TTA `D` leaves the completed wordmark, enters perspective and becomes integrated architectural light inside a finished room.

**Motion law:** identity line -> luminous architectural seam -> spatial threshold -> inhabited room.

**Final hero:** an asymmetrical editorial composition, preferably with a black text field on the left and one dominant room film extending from the axis toward the right. The axis becomes a real alignment boundary rather than decorative residue.

**Media opportunity:** generate a controlled 16:9 room movement from a selected still, beginning near an illuminated ceiling/cove and slowly craning or tilting down into the seating area. Preserve every architectural and material relationship.

**Preferred implementation hypothesis:** DOM/SVG masking plus pre-rendered media. Canvas is rejected unless a small optical seam proves impossible through simpler means.

**Failure condition:** the result becomes an ordinary wipe, a full-screen background video, a glowing sci-fi line, or a forced match between unrelated geometry.

## Territory B — The Room in Three Truths

**Visual thesis:** Three portrait-native architectural planes reveal the finished room, lived use and process intelligence as distinct but coordinated truths.

**Motion law:** spatial procession -> depth relationship -> controlled alignment.

**Final hero:** no card grid. Use no more than three tall planes with one clear dominant plane, black negative space and restrained annotation. The planes behave like walls or spatial leaves, not social-media posts.

**Media opportunity:** use existing portrait videos directly; select one atmosphere plane, one human-use plane and one process plane. Their relationship must remain editorial, not claim that all footage belongs to one project.

**Preferred implementation hypothesis:** DOM/CSS 3D or GSAP-style deterministic transforms. Canvas may be evaluated only if it materially improves bounded depth behavior.

**Failure condition:** generic floating cards, an Awwwards gallery clone, excessive rotation, unreadable overlapping video, or three equal focal points.

## Territory C — The Anamorphic Interior

**Visual thesis:** Several separated image planes align from one privileged camera position into a calm complete room; slight movement reveals the decisions and depth behind the finished impression.

**Motion law:** fragmentation -> perspective registration -> complete spatial image -> restrained separation.

**Final hero:** from the resting camera position, the visitor first sees one authoritative room—not a technical demo. Only deliberate cursor movement or first-scroll progression reveals the spatial planes.

**Media opportunity:** derive multiple exact crops from one selected high-quality room source. Secondary labels may identify light, flow, material, storage or supervision only when supported by evidence.

**Preferred implementation hypothesis:** this is the only territory with a legitimate contained Canvas/WebGL evaluation. Begin with a no-dependency isolated vertical slice. Canvas UI is a source candidate, not an installed entitlement: no external import, CLI, package or upstream code may enter the workspace without separate approval and current source/licence verification.

**Failure condition:** the complete room is never readable, the effect looks like calibration software, pointer motion causes nausea, source resolution collapses, or the canvas replaces semantic content.

## Shared accessibility and performance boundaries

- Core logo, headline, annotation and navigation remain ordinary DOM.
- Reduced motion shows the settled logo briefly and then the final static hero composition with a restrained crossfade.
- Mobile receives a separately composed static or low-motion state; it must not mechanically shrink desktop 3D choreography.
- No study may combine heavy WebGL, an image sequence and multiple autoplay videos.
- Every video has a poster image. Every Canvas state has a static fallback.
- The studies remain private because media rights are unresolved.

## Study decision criteria

1. Specificity to TTA rather than generic interior-design prestige.
2. Strength of the finished room in the first three seconds after transition.
3. Clear relationship between identity, spatial work and motion law.
4. Quiet authority rather than technical showmanship.
5. Honest use of available portrait and process assets.
6. Final still-composition quality.
7. Feasibility, performance, reduced-motion meaning and mobile survival.
8. Whether the direction gives the designer a genuinely new design language rather than repeating previous video/parallax heroes.

## Current gate

All three territories are approved for rough visual study. None is approved for production. Phase 1A logo refinement and hero concept exploration may proceed in parallel, but final logo-to-hero integration waits for the Phase 1A audit verdict.
