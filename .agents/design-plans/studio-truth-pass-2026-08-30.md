## Motion decision record

- **Job:** Let visitors understand five distinct service entry points without interrupting the narrative or reading a dense slide.
- **Evidence:** Services are newly verified and belong immediately before inquiry; the experience needs clarity, not another gallery.
- **Stillness alternative:** A clean editorial service list.
- **Constraints:** Existing React/CSS stack, five short entries, no new dependency, mobile readability, reduced motion, the opening must remain the motion peak.
- **Failure condition:** It feels like tabs, cards, a carousel, a second portfolio, or a scroll trap.

### Candidate comparison

1. **Synchronized dynamic scroll list pinning** — selected and simplified. Borrow the active-zone logic, but use a central registration axis and textual descriptions instead of a product image preview.
2. **SplitText stagger** — rejected as the governing mechanic because the site already uses repeated line reveals; retained only as a restrained masked-line arrival.
3. **Architectural text mask reveal** — rejected because it would create a second cinematic peak and require another full-screen threshold.

### Selected behavior

On desktop the chapter holds while five service names move through a central registration line. The active name sharpens, advances in contrast and reveals one concise description; neighbouring names remain as quiet context. Services, inquiry and footer remain over one continuous narrative material canvas; the registration line completes the handoff without a beige gradient or section seam. There is no image swap, lateral parallax, portal, or card interface.

On mobile and reduced motion, the same content becomes an ordinary vertical editorial list with all descriptions visible.

## Portfolio motion correction

- **Job:** Preserve one dominant residence frame at a time, keep the original photography bright, and make reverse scrolling retrace the same clean motion.
- **Rejected:** The flat overlapping horizontal deck. Its off-axis panels remained perceptible beneath the active image and produced a different-looking reverse-scroll path.
- **Selected:** A simplified cinematic-window reveal. Each incoming project opens vertically inside the same gallery frame while its image counter-moves by less than two percent. The previous project remains the base only until the new aperture fully covers it; then it is removed from visibility.
- **Stillness fallback:** The existing editorial image-and-copy stack used on mobile and reduced motion.
- **Readability treatment:** A restrained local veil over the left copy zone replaces the former heavy half-frame darkening.

## Image decision

No new full-bleed image is required for this pass. The verified service images are too small and inconsistent to support a new cinematic proof chapter without inventing or over-processing evidence. Existing accepted photography remains unchanged; no image-generation call is justified.
