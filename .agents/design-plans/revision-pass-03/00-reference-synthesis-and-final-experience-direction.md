# TTA Revision Pass 03 — Reference Synthesis and Final Experience Direction

## Status and authority

- **Mode:** reference analysis, creative direction, and pre-build planning.
- **Code:** no production behavior is approved or changed by this document.
- **Direct evidence:** the user’s current screenshots and browser annotations; the inspected TTA source structure and current motion logic.
- **Mediated evidence:** `Forensic Website Video Analysis Report.md` and `nice 1.md`. Both describe the same Prosperitaso recording. Neither is a substitute for the original recording.
- **Locked:** the approved Constructed Axis logo opening and the settled hero-room destination.
- **Rejected:** the current hero-to-process beige gradient slab, unsupported blank interludes, and repeated pinned vertical media replacement across adjacent chapters.

## Director verdict

The current page is attractive in fragments but not yet authored as one journey. The opening has identity and atmosphere; the middle repeats one solution—pinned viewport, pale field, left copy, right media, vertical replacement—until Process and Portfolio feel like variations of the same component. Practice adds another low-amplitude vertical parallax. The inquiry and footer are closer to distinct chapters, but their arrival still lacks a controlled narrative handoff.

The correction is not more spectacle. It is a stricter pattern budget, immediate visual proof, and one physical law per chapter.

## Research questions

1. What actually makes the Prosperitaso experience feel coherent despite its many layouts?
2. Which mechanics can improve TTA’s continuity without importing a venture-capital identity or card-heavy architecture?
3. How should the hero hand off to process without a rectangular panel, fade, or empty field?
4. How can Process, Portfolio, Practice, Inquiry, and Footer each feel different while belonging to one material world?
5. Which media assets are required, and which chapters can be solved with the current archive?

## Which report is better?

### Overall winner: `nice 1.md`

`nice 1.md` is the better forensic report because it is more trustworthy about what the recording demonstrates. It explicitly says that only vertical scrolling is visible and that click, hover, drag, and touch interactions are not shown. It describes appearance before naming implementation and keeps mobile behavior unresolved.

### Where the Forensic report is stronger

The Forensic report is the better **idea inventory**. It reconstructs more scenes, identifies more media roles, and gives a more useful catalogue of possible motion families. Its problem is evidentiary inflation: it converts plausible appearance into confident observation and names GSAP, Lenis, ScrollTrigger, hover behavior, form focus, and drag interactions without enough demonstrated proof.

## What the reference is actually teaching us

The strongest transferable principles are:

1. **One shell, many scenes.** A consistent framing, grid, surface language, and type hierarchy make varied chapters feel related.
2. **Register changes mark meaning.** Light, dark, and accent stages are used to announce a narrative shift, not merely for visual variety.
3. **Motion is concentrated.** Ordinary document flow carries many handoffs; pinning and kinetic type are reserved for focal moments.
4. **Media and language arrive together.** Inline imagery, immediate cards, or visible diagrams prevent large statements from becoming unsupported empty screens.
5. **One memorable mechanic per chapter.** The loader, card fan, orbit, pinned type sequence, and final globe do different jobs.
6. **Density alternates with release.** Editorial statements, image proof, grids, and quieter pauses do not all occupy equal visual volume.

The reference does **not** prove that rounded cards, inline image pills, custom cursors, scramble text, or an outer desktop canvas are right for TTA.

## Final visual thesis

### The room remembers the decisions that made it possible.

The page begins with completed atmosphere, travels beneath the finish into site judgment, returns to finished residential evidence and lived use, then resolves into a calm invitation. The same warm material intelligence is present throughout, but every chapter uses a different physical behavior.

This direction is specific to TTA because it joins the studio’s strongest available evidence: refined rooms, early site conditions, close supervision, human use, and a quiet conversation-led inquiry.

## Controlling argument and feeling curve

**Argument:** TTA’s refinement is not decoration applied at the end. It is the visible consequence of decisions made around people, movement, light, services, material, and follow-through.

**Feeling curve:**

`recognition → desire → curiosity → respect → taste → reassurance → trust → invitation`

- **Primary remembered peak:** approved logo-to-hero room reveal.
- **Secondary proof peak:** the first site evidence makes the intelligence beneath the finished room immediately legible.
- **Ending:** the inquiry field settles and withdraws to expose the TTA colophon.

## Section plan 1 — Hero to Process

### Current failure

The current implementation draws a 42vh opaque gradient body across the boundary. It creates a new horizontal slab, then delays the process proposition, producing exactly the dead field the transition was meant to remove.

### Recommended law — Evidence-First Lift

Stop trying to blur the two chapters into one beige fog. The reference shows that ordinary flow and deliberate register changes can feel more controlled than forced seamlessness.

1. The hero completes and holds.
2. On scroll, the hero copy leaves first in two short groups; the room remains fully readable.
3. The entire hero media plane travels upward with the scroll. It does not fade, split, blur away, or scale dramatically.
4. The process scene is already fully composed beneath it: first site image, stage label, proposition, and note. There is no blank introductory panel.
5. A very shallow contact shadow or 2–3svh tonal occlusion may sit under the departing hero plane to establish depth. It must never become a visible colour band.
6. As soon as the boundary crosses the viewport, real evidence is visible. The process proposition never occupies an unsupported full screen.

### Composition

- First process image: approximately 60–68vw by 62–72vh desktop.
- Copy: compact left field or lower-left overlay, no more than 34–38vw.
- First viewport after the hero: at least 35% of the evidence image visible.
- No standalone `Before the finish` screen.

### Copy direction

- Eyebrow: `BEFORE THE FINISH`
- Proposition: `The room is decided while change is still possible.`
- Note: `Layout, light, movement and services are resolved before the finish conceals them.`

Exact wording remains provisional until founder review, but the communication job is fixed.

### Implementation ownership

- Existing DOM/scroll timeline; no new library required.
- Remove the `handoff-gradient` visual entirely.
- Keep hero and process as two physical planes sharing one vertical trajectory.
- No new media is required for this repair.

### Acceptance criteria

- No horizontal colour band or gradient slab is visible.
- No blank field exceeds roughly 15svh before meaningful text or evidence.
- Hero media never fades during the handoff.
- First process evidence shares the viewport with the process proposition on desktop and mobile.
- Reverse scroll restores a coherent hero without ghost surfaces.
- The handoff is quieter than the logo-to-room reveal.

## Section plan 2 — Process: The Registration Room

### Communication job

Prove that TTA reads the room, resolves decisions with people on site, refines surfaces in context, and carries choices through.

### Composition

- One 100svh chamber.
- Asymmetric 32/68 layout.
- One dominant evidence image, not stacked full-screen pages.
- Four concise stage states: `STRUCTURE`, `ALIGNMENT`, `REFINEMENT`, `RESOLUTION`.

### Motion law

Incoming evidence registers from one side through a contained 6–10vw boundary. The boundary may use an SVG displacement or a small fragment shader; the rest of the scene remains stable DOM. Image interiors counter-shift by no more than 3–4%. Copy changes only after the new image is mostly legible.

This is not another vertical parallax. The container does not rise repeatedly, and the main title does not reanimate for every state.

### Assets already available

- `akoka-site-ceiling-open-wide-clean-v1.png`
- `akoka-site-conversation-wide-clean-v1.png`
- `akoka-site-painting-wide-clean-v1.png`
- `akoka-site-installation-wide-clean-v1.png`

### Fallback

Use an authored clip-path registration with no realtime distortion. Mobile becomes four connected vertical evidence chapters.

## Section plan 3 — Selected Residences: Project Constellations

### Current failure

Portfolio repeats the Process architecture: long pin, left copy, right frame, incoming media from below. The first claim is unsupported and the image groups are not clearly organised by project.

### New law — Formation in ordinary flow

- Remove the long portfolio pin.
- Begin with the first project image already visible beside the section proposition.
- Each project uses one dominant room image/film, one material or light detail, and optionally one lived-use fragment.
- As the project enters, the dominant crop relaxes from close to wide; the detail aligns to a real architectural edge; copy resolves after the relationship is legible.
- Between projects, the composition reorganises through measured DOM layout interpolation. No cards fly, no full-screen images stack, and only two planes coexist briefly.

### Required asset audit

Before implementation, group the 125 extracted frames by actual project, room, and evidence role. Colour similarity is not enough. A contact sheet must mark each source as hero-capable, supporting, reject, crop, outpaint, restore, or animate.

### Reference adaptation

The Prosperitaso reference proves that layout variety can remain coherent under one shell. We retain that principle, not its card rows or rounded VC interface.

## Section plan 4 — A Room in Use

### Why this chapter belongs

The current story moves from finished rooms to site practice but lacks a quiet human proof that the space supports life. This is the correct home for the gaming/lived-room footage.

### Composition and motion

- One 70–90svh observation chapter between portfolio and practice.
- Use the lived-room clip full width or as a large cinematic plane with protected copy zone.
- Let the footage play normally. Do not scrub it and do not add another parallax container.
- Copy remains brief: one sentence about rest, hosting, entertainment, movement, or daily use, depending on what the footage honestly shows.

This chapter provides emotional release between designed proof and site authority.

## Section plan 5 — Practice Film

### Final direction

One full-bleed 16:9 site film, not two portrait cards and not another parallax scene.

- Film establishes for 400–700ms.
- Eyebrow enters as a site notation.
- Heading reveals along a real wall or ceiling datum.
- Supporting note appears only after the heading settles.
- The video plays ambiently; scroll controls section arrival, not frame-by-frame playback.

### Asset required later

Create one 6–10 second practice film from the approved site-conversation source. Preserve faces, gestures, ceiling structure, wall openings, and spatial relationships. A stable 16:9 still and poster frame must be approved before animation.

If generated movement drifts, use a 2.5D still. An unstable AI film is worse than stillness.

## Section plan 6 — Inquiry: Living Lacquer

### Communication job

Move from proof to calm readiness. This is the only chapter that should receive a rich realtime material field.

### Visual law

- Broad lacquer-like light body, not particles or generic waves.
- One or two slow refraction forms.
- Delayed, low-amplitude pointer response on capable desktop devices.
- Scroll entry gathers the light toward the headline; exit settles it toward the footer.
- A stable quiet zone protects the heading, CTA, and note.

### Technology

- Custom Three.js fragment shader using the existing project dependency.
- Semantic DOM typography and CTA above the canvas.
- Cap DPR, pause offscreen, dispose resources, and provide a static CSS/material fallback.
- Canvas UI components may be compared in isolation only if they solve this exact material job better; do not import a dramatic demo by default.

### CTA behavior

One aligned horizontal baseline. Label rolls once on hover/focus while the line travels toward an optically aligned arrow. No detached vertical line.

## Section plan 7 — Colophon Underlay

The footer sits beneath the inquiry field. At the final scroll depth, the warm material loses luminance and withdraws upward, revealing a deep espresso colophon.

Information hierarchy:

1. Studio identity and Lagos descriptor.
2. Verified Instagram and inquiry route.
3. Back to top, private-concept note, and verified legal/contact information only.
4. Large TTA wordmark as the final held image.

Do not repeat the main navigation as a footer header. Do not reveal the footer with generic opacity plus Y-translation.

## Typography direction

The reference’s serif/sans pairing should not be copied automatically. Beige plus editorial serif would push TTA toward generic luxury and away from the precision of its logo.

Recommended direction:

- One expressive editorial grotesk for major headings.
- One highly legible text face or the same family for body copy.
- A restrained engineered accent for process labels only.
- Short line masks for true chapter headings; no per-letter choreography across long sentences.

Prototype candidates must be tested with real TTA copy and verified web licences. `Instrument Sans` is a credible open-source baseline for the specimen; commercial alternatives such as Neue Montreal/Suisse/Diatype remain candidates only if licensing is resolved. Bahnschrift may be tested only as a minor technical accent.

Chapter exceptions:

- Hero-to-process proposition: arrives with evidence, mostly still.
- Process stage words: precise registration change.
- Portfolio title: static while composition forms.
- Practice heading: architectural datum reveal.
- Inquiry heading: universal line-mask family.
- Footer wordmark: uncovered, not animated like a heading.

## Surface system

The page should not use one WebGL background behind everything. Coherence comes from a material progression:

1. deep warm logo field;
2. photographic hero atmosphere;
3. warm technical process chamber;
4. quieter editorial residence surface;
5. dark observed practice film;
6. responsive warm inquiry material;
7. deep espresso colophon.

The same invisible grid, type roles, warm-black text, measured captions, and photographic grade connect these registers. Section boundaries may be deliberate; they do not need fake gradients.

## Mobile law

- No fixed landscape canvas.
- Every abstract proposition shares a viewport with evidence.
- Long desktop pins become ordinary vertical chapters.
- Project formations become one dominant image plus one detail.
- Realtime displacement becomes authored clip states on low-power devices.
- The practice chapter may use the original portrait source if it preserves the human action better.
- Inquiry shader reduces resolution and pointer response; all information remains accessible without Canvas.

## Reduced-motion law

The complete argument must survive as:

`settled logo → hero room → process proposition plus first image → four static process chapters → static project spreads → lived-use poster → practice poster → inquiry still → readable colophon`

## Final acceptance test

The page is resolved only when a cold-scroll viewer can describe the experience without naming effects:

> TTA first lets me feel the room, then shows me the decisions and people behind it, proves the result in real spaces and daily life, and ends by inviting a serious conversation.

If the viewer instead remembers gradients, cards, parallax, shaders, or repeated heading animations, the system has failed.
