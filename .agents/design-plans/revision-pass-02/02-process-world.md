# Process World Redesign

## Comments answered

Browser comments 7, 8, and 9.

## Current failure

The warm Canvas surface is too uniform, the image window is polite, and the image replacements share a simple upward entry. The background behaves like a theme colour rather than a place. Text, image, and Canvas are adjacent, but they do not appear governed by one spatial system.

## Communication job

Prove that TTA’s finished atmosphere is supported by early spatial judgment, on-site alignment, surface refinement, and follow-through.

This scene should feel more disciplined and technical than the hero, while still belonging to the same warm material world.

## Recommended direction — The Registration Room

### Spatial composition

- Create one dedicated 100svh pinned chamber.
- Use an asymmetric `32 / 68` composition: concise copy and annotations on the left, dominant evidence media on the right.
- Increase the image plane to approximately 62–70vw and 68–76vh on desktop.
- Remove ornamental grid lines. Use only one real alignment datum inherited from the logo or room joinery.
- Let the background carry low-frequency material behavior: grazing light, plaster/lacquer depth, and a soft moving shadow. It must not resemble animated noise.

### Evidence sequence

1. Open ceiling / structure.
2. Site conversation / alignment.
3. Painting or finish testing / refinement.
4. Installation or resolved room / follow-through.

The sequence proves process. It must never claim these images are the construction history of the separate hero room.

### Image-change law

Each incoming image is **registered**, not stacked like a card:

1. A low-amplitude material boundary travels upward through the existing image.
2. Behind that boundary, the next image is already aligned to the same architectural frame.
3. The internal image moves 4–7% in the opposite direction, creating depth without making the container feel like a carousel.
4. The active caption and annotation change only after the new image reaches at least 70% visibility.

The boundary may be implemented as an SVG/noise displacement mask or a small contained fragment shader. The distortion belongs to the image edge only; text and controls remain ordinary DOM.

## Alternative direction — The Site Table

Treat each process asset as a large physical sheet on an architect’s review table. The active sheet remains dominant; the next sheet slides from beneath at a slight offset; notes pin to stable outer edges.

### Strength

Lower implementation risk and clearer reverse-scroll behavior.

### Risk

If shadows, rotations, or paper metaphors become obvious, it will feel like a portfolio template rather than interior space.

## Canvas and library decision

- **Cinematic Window:** retain only the opposite-motion principle between frame and image. Reject its literal three-column window layout.
- **Synchronized Dynamic Scroll List Pinning:** useful for mapping active copy to evidence, but the visible left side should be concise annotations, not a long list.
- **Fluid/displacement Canvas UI:** conditionally suitable only for the image-change boundary. Before any upstream import, verify exact component, licence, framework target, mobile fallback, and mutation. A small custom shader may be safer because Three.js is already present.
- **Spatial Convergence:** reject for process. Flying fragments would make serious site evidence feel theatrical.

## Copy system

Use three levels only:

1. **Stage word:** `STRUCTURE`, `ALIGNMENT`, `REFINEMENT`, `RESOLUTION`.
2. **Human instruction:** `Read the room`, `Resolve it together`, `Let light test the finish`, `Carry every decision through`.
3. **One practical note:** 10–16 words describing the visible decision.

Optional persistent marginal note:

`The visible finish inherits every decision made before it.`

Do not add long paragraphs, invented process stages, statistics, or project claims.

## Type behavior

- Stage words change with a precise horizontal registration shift or optical cut.
- Main instructions use the approved headline specimen, but remain mostly still while media changes.
- Practical notes reveal after the image, not simultaneously.

## Mobile translation

- Replace the long pin with four vertically connected evidence chapters.
- Each chapter shows stage word, title, one image, and one note together.
- A short sticky stage label may persist, but images must remain reachable without precision scrolling.
- No realtime displacement on low-power mobile; use an authored clip-path reveal or ordinary image replacement.

## Reduced motion

Use discrete evidence states with no internal parallax. The stage word, image, title, and note change as one coherent group.

## Acceptance tests

- The background feels materially alive even when the images are hidden.
- The media is unmistakably the dominant proof.
- Every image change reads as one controlled physical law.
- Copy never competes with an image boundary in motion.
- Reverse scroll does not create absurd liquid/gravity behavior.
- The scene remains readable with Canvas disabled.
