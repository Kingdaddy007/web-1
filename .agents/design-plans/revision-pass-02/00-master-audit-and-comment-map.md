# TTA Revision Pass 02 — Master Audit and Comment Map

## Status and boundary

- **Mode:** design diagnosis and pre-build planning.
- **Live code:** unchanged by this planning pass.
- **Prototype truth:** private speculative outreach concept, not an approved public website.
- **Locked:** approved logo construction and the current hero arrival through the settled room.
- **Open:** hero-to-process handoff, process world, portfolio proof, practice media, inquiry treatment, footer architecture, typography system, and later copy refinement.

## Director verdict

The page has a strong opening and several attractive moments, but the middle and ending were designed as independent sections. Repeated vertical reveals, pale warm surfaces, pinned compositions, and large sans-serif statements are being used as a universal solution. The result is motion without enough difference in physical law.

The next pass must not add more effects indiscriminately. It must give each chapter a different communication job while preserving one coherent material world.

## The six design problems

1. **Continuity:** The hero gives way to a new beige panel instead of transforming into the process world.
2. **Process authority:** The process scene feels like content placed on a canvas, not a serious environment with spatial pressure.
3. **Portfolio evidence:** A claim appears before visible proof, then unrelated images repeat one transition law.
4. **Practice media:** Two portrait videos describe the source archive rather than creating the full-bleed cinematic experience requested.
5. **Closing architecture:** The inquiry shader is too quiet to read as an authored material event, while the footer resembles a second header.
6. **Typography:** The declared font stack falls back to Arial on this Windows machine because Helvetica Neue and Inter are not installed.

## New motion grammar

The page should use one law per narrative job:

| Chapter | Job | Physical law | Motion track |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Logo and hero | Recognition and desire | Material registration and camera discovery | Arrival |
| Hero to process | Shift from atmosphere to intelligence | The same room changes focus and direction before it becomes a process surface | Scroll-bound |
| Process | Prove early decisions and supervision | Evidence registers, locks, and is replaced inside one architectural frame | Scroll-bound |
| Portfolio | Show taste and coherent project depth | One project composes itself from room, detail, and lived-use evidence | Scroll-bound/editorial |
| Practice | Human authority and proximity | One full-bleed site film holds while copy enters in measured layers | Ambient plus short scroll arrival |
| Inquiry | Slow the visitor into readiness | Warm material light bends and settles around the invitation | Ambient/interaction |
| Footer | Closure and identity | Inquiry material withdraws; the colophon is uncovered beneath it | Scroll-bound threshold |

## What is deliberately rejected

- One repeated `translateY + opacity` reveal for every heading and every section.
- Another vertical stack of full-screen cards for portfolio work.
- A 3D camera portal built without an actual 3D room model.
- Two portrait videos presented as equal practice cards.
- Gaming footage as the final proof of “resolved atmosphere.”
- A footer containing the same Work / Practice / Inquiry row as the header.
- A page-wide Canvas replacement for semantic HTML.
- Importing Canvas UI or any external font before source, licence, fallback, and mutation approval.

## Approval sequence

1. Approve one hero-to-process continuity treatment.
2. Approve one process-world composition and image-change law.
3. Complete a project-family asset audit before approving portfolio choreography.
4. Produce one practice-film source frame and 16:9 test.
5. Compare two contained inquiry material slices.
6. Compare three typography specimens using the same real page content.
7. Only then implement a revised vertical slice from hero through footer.

## Primary remembered peak and ending

- **Primary peak:** the logo/material world resolves into the complete hero room.
- **Secondary proof peak:** process evidence makes the room’s intelligence legible.
- **Ending impression:** calm readiness to begin a serious conversation.

Later effects must not become louder than the opening.
