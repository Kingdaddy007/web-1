## Communication job

Reframe work currently experienced through portrait social media as a controlled cinematic spatial identity. The opening should make TTA recognize its own room and then perceive that room with greater authority.

## Opening micro-story

1. **Identity:** The approved Constructed Axis arrival resolves the exact TTA wordmark.
2. **Threshold:** The wordmark withdraws as one coordinated identity state while its vertical axis remains.
3. **Reframing:** The axis opens into a narrow spatial slit and then a cinematic aperture.
4. **Authority:** One TTA room becomes the sole focal point and holds without labels or interface.
5. **Invitation downward:** First scroll expands the aperture toward the near-full-bleed state and begins the project journey.

## Static candidates

1. **Centered 2.39:1 aperture:** Most cinematic and most direct continuation from the central axis. Risk: ordinary letterbox/video-player association.
2. **Asymmetric architectural aperture:** Stronger spatial tension and less literal cinema language. Risk: empty black must remain structurally intentional.
3. **Near full bleed:** Strongest room authority and intended first-scroll destination. Risk: loses the threshold idea if used as the initial resting frame.

## Recommended choreography pairing

Use the centered or asymmetric aperture as the autoplay arrival destination. Use near full bleed as the first-scroll state. Do not make the 2.39:1 letterbox the permanent page template.

## Implementation comparison after static selection

1. Baseline: DOM image plus CSS/SVG clipping.
2. Media option: pre-rendered short room move when the still image cannot provide sufficient camera life.
3. Canvas option: one contained refractive seam around the axis during 3.70–4.60s only.

Canvas must not own the room, text, controls, or complete hero. It proceeds only if a thin optical refraction communicates the threshold materially better than the baseline and retains a static/reduced-motion fallback.
