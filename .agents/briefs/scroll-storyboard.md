## Motion law

- Native scroll remains in control.
- Rendered progress interpolates briefly toward actual scroll progress; there is no scroll lock or global smooth-scroll dependency.
- The hero and process field travel vertically. No centre split, registration seam, portal, or lateral wipe is allowed.
- The proof images move only inside their restrained media window and counter-travel slightly for depth.
- During the breath, a grazing lacquer light travels across the material while the headline resolves character by character from embossed low contrast into espresso ink.
- The canvas owns only light and material behavior. The headline remains semantic DOM text, and one shared CSS progress value composites the complete letter sequence.
- Mobile preserves the same three beats with copy above the evidence window.
- Reduced motion switches directly between the same semantic states.
- The portfolio handoff is one pinned chapter: `What was decided becomes what is felt.` physically recedes while five finished residential moments process upward one at a time.
- The practice uses two real autoplay-muted films and pauses them offscreen. They counter-travel slowly rather than scrubbing.
- The inquiry canvas is a contained, low-power WebGL material field. It pauses offscreen, caps device pixel ratio at 1.5, and becomes still for reduced motion.
- All visible numeric chapter labels are removed. The narrative hierarchy is carried by language and transition, not editorial numbering.
- The inquiry is a prototype state. It does not submit data or claim a verified contact route.
